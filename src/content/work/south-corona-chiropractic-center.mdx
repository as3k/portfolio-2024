# South Corona Chiropractic Center

When Dr. Torrez purchased South Corona Chiropractic, he needed a new website to represent his practice and attract local patients. The previous website belonged to the office's former owner, and with outdated design and little usable content, we started from scratch to create a modern, user-friendly experience.

## Challenge

Starting a new practice required:
- Building a website from scratch with no existing content
- Attracting local patients in a competitive market
- Making appointment booking as easy as possible
- Conveying the welcoming, professional atmosphere of the practice
- Competing with established chiropractors in Corona, CA

## Goals

The primary goal was to **drive appointment scheduling**, so I incorporated an online booking system prominently across the site. To support local visibility, we conducted extensive on-page SEO targeting Corona, CA, and the surrounding areas.

## Process

I handled every aspect of the project, from design and development to branding and content strategy, working closely with Dr. Torrez to refine the layout, visuals, and copy. Using Next.js and Vercel for performance and scalability, the website provided a seamless experience for visitors while capturing the practice's fresh and natural approach to chiropractic care.

Photography of the office further added a personal touch, showcasing the space while keeping the focus on Dr. Torrez and his personalized care.

### Through this project, I:

- **Designed and developed a modern website**, building from the ground up to reflect the practice's mission and attract local patients.
- **Implemented an online booking system** prominently across the site, making it easy for visitors to schedule appointments.
- **Optimized for local SEO**, targeting Corona, CA, and surrounding areas to improve search rankings and visibility.
- **Collaborated with Dr. Torrez** to refine the website's design, layout, and copy, ensuring it aligned with his vision for the practice.
- **Captured original photography**, showcasing the welcoming and professional atmosphere of the office.

## Results

The design reflected the welcoming and professional environment of Dr. Torrez's office, emphasizing accessibility, expertise, and patient care.

- **25% increase in scheduling** - Online booking drove more appointments
- **Huge increase in usability** - Modern, intuitive design
- **Drove targeted local traffic** - Effective local SEO implementation
- **Lightning fast load time** - Next.js optimization
- **40+ photos taken** - Custom photography showcasing the practice
