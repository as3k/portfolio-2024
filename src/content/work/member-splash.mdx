## The platform

Member Splash is membership management software for swim clubs. 460+ clubs use it to run online registration, collect dues, manage waitlists, and coordinate what makes a season work. The platform runs on WordPress Multisite with a custom plugin architecture.

I'm the product engineer. I own features from problem framing through production. The specifics are under NDA, but I can speak to the shape of the work.

## What I've shipped

**A member credential system** that rolled out to all 460+ clubs. No opt-in migration. Every existing membership got updated automatically. The core engineering challenge was integrating across several subsystems that weren't designed to talk to each other.

**A check-in flow redesign** that brought check-in time from 30+ seconds to under 10. The previous flow had too many steps between "person shows up" and "attendance recorded." I cut the friction. Tested with actual club staff before shipping. Used the 30-second baseline as the benchmark throughout.

**A Docker-based development environment** that replaced a fragile, documentation-heavy local setup. Onboarding a new developer stopped being a half-day project. Local testing became the default instead of something people skipped.

**Security remediation** after a third-party audit surfaced vulnerabilities in the codebase. Triaged by exploitability and blast radius. Addressed critical issues first. Documented the patterns that caused the problems, not just the individual findings. Closing tickets isn't the goal. Not reopening them is.

## What I'm after

Every one of these is the same job underneath. Figure out what's broken. Figure out why it matters. Build the thing that fixes the real problem, not the visible symptom.

I own it from design through implementation. That's the part I don't outsource.
