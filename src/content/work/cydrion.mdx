# Cydrion

Cydrion needed a complete digital presence to establish its position as a leader in ISP support. As a fresh build, I had the opportunity to shape the website and brand identity from the ground up, crafting an experience that not only showcased their services but also reflected their innovative and reliable approach to ISP support.

## Challenge

As a new company in the ISP support space, Cydrion needed:
- A complete brand identity built from scratch
- Clear communication of technical services to a wide audience
- Visual language that conveyed both innovation and reliability
- A website that positioned them as industry leaders

## Process

I led the design and development of Cydrion's website, creating a cohesive digital identity that highlighted their expertise in network operations and customer support. My work spanned branding, iconography, voice and tone, and all visual elements to ensure a consistent and engaging experience for users.

### Through this process, I:

- **Designed a bold and modern brand identity** that positioned Cydrion as a trustworthy and innovative partner for ISPs.
- **Created custom iconography** to visually communicate technical concepts like proactive monitoring and rapid response.
- **Developed a clear and approachable voice and tone** to make complex services accessible to a wide audience.
- **Built a responsive, visually striking, and performance-optimized website** that emphasizes Cydrion's core offerings, including NOC and CSM support.
- **Crafted action-driven CTAs** to guide users to connect with the team, request services, or learn more about Cydrion's solutions.

## Results

- **92% Accessibility Score** - Ensuring the site is usable for all visitors
- **0.6s load time** - Lightning-fast performance for technical audiences
- **Market-aligned brand identity** - Positioned Cydrion as a credible leader in ISP support
- **Clear value proposition** - Complex services made accessible through thoughtful design
