## Context

This is a recruiting-focused portfolio rebuild, not a visual reskin. It must establish technical and product credibility while keeping claims accurate, concise, and grounded in Zachary’s actual work.

## Goals

- Make the primary target role clear within the first ten seconds.
- Lead with evidence, decisions, and outcomes rather than self-description.
- Show ownership across disciplines without implying that collaboration is unnecessary.
- Separate confirmed facts from unknown or confidential information.
- Preserve direct, calm, technically literate voice.

## Non-goals

- Renaming historical job titles.
- Publishing proprietary systems, customer data, security incident details, or Pacific Life information before explicit approval.
- Treating AI as the primary professional differentiator.
- Turning every project into a long engineering article.

## Decisions

- **Positioning:** Use Design Engineer as the public headline and UX Engineer as adjacent positioning. Supporting language describes product design, frontend engineering, systems thinking, and cross-functional delivery.
- **Evidence standard:** Publish only confirmed information. Identify internal timing, qualitative feedback, estimates, and modeled outcomes honestly.
- **Case-study model:** Use a reusable structure: context, problem, role and collaboration, constraints/evidence, exploration and decisions, implementation where relevant, validation, outcome, and reflection.
- **Project hierarchy:** Member Splash and PXC become differentiated flagships. Pacific Life appears only after its anonymized evidence is reconstructed. Supporting projects remain useful but visually subordinate.
- **Technical changes:** Security/privacy fixes remain separately reviewable work items, even when discovered during this portfolio rebuild.

## Risks and mitigations

- **Claim drift** → Maintain `PORTFOLIO-REBUILD.md` as the fact and decision record.
- **Confidentiality** → Keep sensitive implementation and customer data out of public copy; request explicit publication approval for measurements.
- **Scope creep** → Complete P0 credibility corrections and flagship narratives before polish or new visuals.
- **Recruiter confusion** → Test each major surface against Design Engineering, UX Engineering, Product Design, and frontend/product engineering expectations.

## Validation

- Run lint and production build once dependencies are available.
- Manually test responsive layouts, keyboard navigation, focus states, reduced motion, links, metadata, structured data, sitemap, and robots.
- Conduct a final four-lens hiring-manager review before declaring the rebuild complete.
